Special Destination: China

Terracotta Army, Xi’an

If we had visited Xi’an, we would have seen the Terracotta Warriors. (Third Conditional)

Grammar Focus: Conditionals

First Conditional: Real possibility in the future. (If + present simple, will + verb)

Second Conditional: Hypothetical present or future. (If + past simple, would + verb)

Third Conditional: Imaginary past. (If + past perfect, would have + past participle)
